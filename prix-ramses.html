Meet the Jury

Projects submitted to the Prix RAMSES are evaluated by a jury composed of professionals and experts from the fields of architecture, urban planning, design, construction, and territorial development. The jury's role is to identify the most remarkable projects based on several criteria: Originality, Feasibility, and Impact.
