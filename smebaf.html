Destinations

France

France is recognized for the excellence of its Grandes Écoles and the diversity of its programs . It is also an affordable destination in public education: public universities are heavily subsidized by the State, maintaining low tuition fees even for international students .

Cost of living ~€700 – €1,200/month

Belgium

Belgium offers high-quality higher education in a bilingual framework (French/Dutch) at the heart of European institutions. Fees are competitive and the cost of living is moderate compared to neighbors .

Cost of living ~€800 – €1,300/month

Germany

German public universities generally do not charge tuition fees for bachelor's programs and many master's programs. Academic quality is high and ties with industry are strong.

FAQs

01

Is SMEBAF an immigration fair?

No. SMEBAF is strictly an educational and academic mobility fair focusing on higher education studies, institutional exchange, and academic guidance.

02

Can I come without prior registration?

Welcome and registration of participants take place on site from 08:00 AM to 09:00 AM. Admission is open to students, pupils, parents, and professionals.

03

What is the selection process like?

Meet directly with university representatives, present your academic background, and receive personalized evaluations on admission requirements.

04

Is the information provided reliable?

Yes, all universities, embassies, and consular agencies present are officially accredited and recognized.

05

Where can I get tickets

Tickets and access badges are available through Eventify online registration and directly at the venue entrance.
